# Quaise Energy

> Deep-geothermal energy company and MIT spinout developing millimeter-wave (MMW) drilling — using a
> gyrotron to vaporize rather than grind rock — to reach supercritical conditions several kilometers
> underground and convert existing fossil-fuel power stations to superhot geothermal baseload.

Quaise Energy is an energy and hardware company, not a software vendor. **It publishes no public API,
no developer portal, no SDK, and no machine-readable API contract of any kind.** This file is generated
by API Evangelist from publicly reachable pages; it exists so an agent can stop looking for a developer
surface that does not exist, rather than repeatedly crawling for one.

- Founded: 2018, spun out of the MIT Plasma Science and Fusion Center
- Founders: Carlos Araque, Matthew Houde; core research by Paul Woskov (MIT)
- Backers: Vinod Khosla (initial), The Engine (MIT's venture fund, seed round)
- Milestone: 100 meters drilled with millimeter-wave technology at its Central Texas field site (2025)

## Tools
- [LCOE Calculator](https://www.quaise.com/lcoe/calculator): A client-side React application that models
  levelized cost of energy. Full functionality requires an account — sign-in is handled by AWS Cognito at
  [/lcoe/calculator/login](https://www.quaise.com/lcoe/calculator/login). This is an end-user web tool, not
  an API: it exposes no documented endpoints, no key issuance, and no published contract.
